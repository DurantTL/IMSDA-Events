The club portal header: white masthead, navy club banner and a single flat tab row with every club tool.

- Ten tabs, in order: Home, Roster, Forms, Club events, Honors, Class tracking, Monthly Records, Orders, Health, Club settings. The row never scrolls sideways or is cut off. With room it is the flat strip. Below 1024px, or when the strip would not fit, it becomes a menu line: the current tab (3px `--imsda-purple` underline) and a **Club menu** button (**Coordinator menu** on the Area Coordinator's tabs) that opens the full list as a sheet or dialog, the current item marked with `aria-current` and a purple left edge. On a phone (800px and below) the club's own pages swap the top line for a bottom bar of plain links: Home, Roster, Events (Club events), Records (Monthly Records) and **More** (only when the role has more than five tabs; it lists the rest). A role with five tabs or fewer, such as a Reporter, gets just its tabs. Area Coordinator pages have no bottom bar.
- Tabs are 48px tall, `--muted` text; the current tab has `aria-current="page"` (the bar and the list say `"true"`, so a page has one "page"), `--imsda-navy` text and a 3px `--imsda-purple` underline.
- Masthead: the IMAdventist symbol with "Club portal" (coordinators see "Club coordination"), the signed-in name and role, and 44px outlined actions: **Your account**, **Forms help**, **Sign out** (plus **Switch club** for people with more than one club).
- **Approved Nov 9, 2026 (not built yet):** on phones the masthead is one row and the banner one line, the four outlined masthead actions fold into one **Account** button, and the Area Coordinator portal uses the same five-slot bottom bar as the club portal in place of its "Coordinator menu" line. See `PhoneHeader`.
- The navy banner names the club, its church and the person's role, with the club year in an outlined box on the right.
