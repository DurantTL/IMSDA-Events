IMAdventist: quick start for club leaders

For club directors, deputies and Area Coordinators · November 2026

Start at events.imsda.org. IMAdventist (Events & Club Ministry) keeps club records and event registrations together. Directors keep their club's information up to date. Area Coordinators review progress and follow up with directors. The roster records who is in the club. An event registration records who is going to one event.

No earlier training needed. This handout assumes you are new to the system. It covers signing in, everyday club tasks and where to get help.

Sign in and open your work

  1. On the website, choose Sign in. Use your account email and password, or Sign in with a passkey if you set one up. If a Security check box appears and your browser blocks it, choose Email me a code instead.
  2. On Confirm it's you, enter the six-digit code from your authenticator app or a recovery code, or use your passkey. Lost your phone? Choose Recover two-step sign-in.
  3. Director or deputy: choose My club (or My clubs) in the account menu, then the right club. Area Coordinator: choose Club coordination.
  4. Check the club name and club year at the top before you change anything. Your role decides which tabs appear.

First time with a club invitation?

Use the email address that received the invitation. Already have an account? Sign in to it. New here? Choose Create an account, enter your details, then enter the six-digit code we email you. Then accept the club invitation on your account page.

Club pages ask you to turn on two-step sign-in once. A passkey (your fingerprint, face or PIN) is marked Easiest; an authenticator app also works. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe.

The emailed code confirms your email address. Two-step sign-in protects club records about young people. An Area Coordinator role comes from the conference office.

Club director or deputy: everyday tasks

I need to… Go here and do this
See what needs attention Home. Check the club year, the year summary and the Getting started list. Each step has a Help guide.
Add a member Roster → Add to roster. Search first so you don't add someone twice. Enter the details, choose Add to roster and check the new row. This does not register anyone for an event.
Correct or remove a member Edit beside the name, then Save. Actions holds Deactivate and Remove. Remove erases roster details and cannot be undone.
Send a roster to an outside event Roster → Export builder. Pick and order columns, Preview, Download CSV. Save the layout with a name for next time.
Complete a club form Forms. Fill in yourself, or Send link so a parent fills it in. A saved draft is not a submitted form.
Enter a member's health record Roster → Actions → Health record. The first time, read the privacy notice, tick I have read this and I agree and choose Continue. Enter the record from the paper form, or Send link so a parent fills it in (the link works once). The status shows No record yet, Needs update or Current for this club year.
Keep a health record current When the status says Needs update and nothing has changed, choose Confirm this record is still correct for this club year. Otherwise choose Edit this record.
See health information for an event Health. Choose the event to see your club's attendees: dietary notes as entered, a yes/no medical-need flag and emergency contacts. Open printable sheet to print it. Each event is open through its last day.
Record honors Honors. Add beside a name, or Add honors to several members.
Record a meeting Monthly Records → Add meeting note. Enter the date, counts and what you did.
Submit the month's report Monthly Records → Open or Continue report. Check the counts, type your full name and the date under Signature, then Submit report. Save draft does not file it. Reports are due the 10th of the next month.
Register for an event Club events → Register. Choose who is going (Select everyone or tick people), continue through the details, review, and submit. Check for Your club is registered and a confirmation number. A draft or waitlist place is not a confirmed registration.
Record a finished class Class tracking. Select the members, set the date, then Mark Friend completed (the button names the class you chose). Then choose Add to order list for the suggested insignia, or Not now.
Order and hand out items Orders. The order list is a helper, not an official order form; you still order from AdventSource. Keep supplies on hand up to date. When you give an item to a member, tick them and choose Mark handed out; the app lowers your stock for you.
File the Year-End Report Monthly Records → Year-End Report → Open report. Most counts fill in from your roster, honors and classes. Save draft until ready. It covers May 1 to April 30 and is due April 1. Submitting locks it until conference staff reopen it.

A deputy has the director's tools. A registrar or reporter has fewer, so some tabs may be missing for them.

Health information is confidential. Only the director and deputy can open it. A registrar can enter a record when none exists yet, but can't read it back. Every time you open, enter, change or print health information, the app records your name and the time. Health records are never emailed or exported. Don't copy, photograph or share them.

Area Coordinator: review and follow up

I need to… Go here and do this
See which clubs need help Club coordination → Overview. Who's missing what lists each club that is behind with one next step. The app does not send messages for you.
Find a club Overview → Clubs at a glance. Search by club, church or city, then Open club. Club pages open View only.
Read a Monthly Record Monthly Records. Pick a month. Submitted means filed; Not submitted means the month ended without a filed record. Open a submitted record to read it.
Understand points Points. Report points plus on-time yearly registration points. The full-year points CSV covers every club.
Check event participation Club events. Each club's status and headcount. Only registered clubs count toward the event total. You can't register a club or change a registration.
Decide a team request Team approvals. Decide adult team members (18 or older) when a club asks.
Read a new-club application New clubs. Read the details. A system administrator approves or declines.
Check waitlists Waitlists (inside Club coordination).

Most coordinator work is view only. The director keeps the roster and club settings, so ask the director for any change.

Everyday habits

  • Leave without saving? appears if you try to leave a form with changes you haven't saved. Choose Keep editing to go back.
  • Search boxes filter as you type. Select a column header to sort a table. Clear filters shows everything again.
  • Status words mean the same thing everywhere: Draft is started but not submitted; Submitted, Confirmed, Waitlisted and Cancelled are the other common states.

If you need help

  • Forms help is at the top of every club page. Help in the account menu has short guides and Step by step guides with a picture of each step.
  • Forgot password? Choose Forgot password? on the sign-in page and follow the emailed code.
  • Club or coordinator tools missing? Check which email you signed in with. Ask the conference office to check your invitation or role. Please don't create a second account.
  • Something won't save? Contact support@imsda.org or (515) 223-1197 with the club, the page, what you were trying to do and any message you saw. Leave passwords, codes, private links and health details out of the message.

Before you leave, check that your work saved, then choose Sign out, especially on a shared computer.
