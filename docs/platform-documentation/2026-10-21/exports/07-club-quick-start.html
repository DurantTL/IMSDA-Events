IMSDA Events: quick start for club leaders

A practical guide for club directors and Area Coordinators · October 2026
Start at events.imsda.org. Use the tools assigned to your account.

For club directors, deputies and Area Coordinators · October 2026

Start at events.imsda.org. IMSDA Events keeps club records and event participation together. Directors maintain their club's information; coordinators review progress and follow up with directors. The roster records club membership. An event registration records who is going to one event.

Sign in and open your work

  1. On the website, choose Sign in. Use your own existing account email and password, or a configured passkey when offered.
  2. Complete Confirm it's you if requested. Use your authenticator, an unused recovery code or the passkey option offered.
  3. Director/deputy: open My club or My clubs, then the correct club. Area Coordinator: use Clubs overview on the Area coordinator card, or Clubs in account navigation.
  4. Check the club/year or year heading before working. Your actual assignment determines which tools appear.

First time with a club invitation?

Use the email address that received the invitation. Already have an account? Sign in to it. New here? Choose Create an account, enter your details and Create account. Keep the same browser open; enter the emailed six-digit code and choose Verify and continue. Then choose Accept beside the correct club invitation on your account page.

If prompted, complete Set up two-step sign-in. With an authenticator, choose Set up an authenticator, follow the displayed setup instructions, enter Code from your authenticator and Confirm and turn on. Save the recovery codes and choose I've saved these codes, then Continue. A passkey is another supported method when offered; both methods are not required.

The email verification code and authenticator code do different jobs. The first confirms your email address; the second protects access to club information. An Area Coordinator assignment comes from the conference office rather than a public role selector.

Club director or deputy: practical tasks

I need to… Go here and do this
See what needs attention My club → Home. Check the club name/year, next task and summaries.
Add a regular member Roster → Add to roster. Check for an existing row first, enter the requested details and confirm the saved row. This does not register the person for an event.
Correct or pause a member Use the person's Edit control and Save. Deactivate keeps the person on file; Reactivate returns them to the active list. Read Remove carefully: it erases roster details and cannot be undone.
Complete a club form Forms → available form. Fill in for a member or use the private-link option. Return to Filled forms; use Open for a draft or View and print for a submitted record.
Record a meeting Monthly Records → month → Add meeting note. Enter the date, counts and details; save. Optional named attendance depends on roster access.
Submit the month's report Open the month and Open Monthly Record or Continue report. Check the details and displayed due date. Save draft is not submission; use Submit report when ready and check the submitted result. The current due date is the 10th of the next month.
Register for an event Club events → Register / Continue. Follow Who's going → Each person's details → Review. Submit and check the confirmation/status. A saved draft or waitlist is not a confirmed place.
Check an existing registration Club events → View. Make permitted edits with Save changes while available. Use Print club packet or Download registration PDF where offered. For a church-billed event, follow the sponsoring church's office billing process.
Review achievements or supplies Honors, Class tracking and Orders cover their supported records, helper lists, stock and hand-outs. Orders is not an online store checkout. Use the available approved reports/exports when needed.

A deputy has the director's capabilities. A registrar and reporter have narrower roles, so their available tools differ. A missing task may need an assignment check rather than a second account.

Area Coordinator: review and follow up

I need to… Go here and do this
Find a club Clubs → All clubs. Search by club, church or city; choose Open. Other clubs normally open View only.
Choose a follow-up task Overview. Check the year shown, reports, points and summary counts. Open the club to understand the item. Reading a summary does not complete its task.
Read a monthly report Monthly Records. Find the club/month. Select a submitted month's points to open its report. Draft is started but not submitted; Missing means the due date passed without a submitted record.
Understand points Points. Review the chart/table and separate components; sort by points or club name. Download points CSV covers the full year's authorized club list, not just the visible name search.
Check event participation Club events. Read each club's state and headcount. Registered, Draft, Waitlisted and Cancelled mean different things. Follow up with the director about the actual unfinished item.
Review an assigned team request Team approvals. Read the event's rules and use Approve or Decline only for the requests your own coordinator account may decide.
Review a new-club application New clubs. Coordinators may read the information; a system administrator makes the approval/decline decision.

The current coordinator list shows available active clubs; an enforced “my area” selector is not part of this guide. Most coordinator work is oversight, with the specific Team approvals exception. Directors make ordinary corrections to their own club's records.

If you need help

  • Forgot password? Choose Forgot password? → Send me a code. In the same browser, enter the emailed code and new password, then Set new password.
  • Club or coordinator tools missing? Check the signed-in email. Ask the conference office to verify the invitation/assignment; avoid making a duplicate account or new club application.
  • Lost authenticator? Use a saved recovery code or available passkey. Ask the office for account support if neither is available.
  • Page or save problem? Tell support the club/year or event, page, intended action and visible error. Keep passwords, codes, private links and member health details out of messages/screenshots.

Before leaving, confirm the result of your task and choose Sign out, especially on a shared device. Next time, return to the same website and use your existing account.
