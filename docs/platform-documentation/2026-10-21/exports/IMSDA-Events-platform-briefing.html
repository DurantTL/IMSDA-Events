WHAT IT DOES
Why we use IMSDA Events
Keep club information together
Maintain the roster, club forms, meeting records and achievements in one place.
Reuse the roster for events
Choose the people attending instead of starting a new list each time.
See what needs attention
Directors see their next tasks. Coordinators review records and participation.
Return to the saved record
Find the report, registration, choices or approved working copy again.
