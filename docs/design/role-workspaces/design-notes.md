# IMSDA Events: four distinct role experiences

Prepared 20 October 2026, America/Chicago. Source boundaries were checked against `8eb81d7d`; current main remains `72a6cb21`.

This replaces the earlier PDF's presentation of role layouts. The earlier review's findings still stand; its shared staff-style shell did not make the participant, director and coordinator experiences sufficiently distinct.

## The proposed distinction

| Role | Workspace | Primary scope and work |
|---|---|---|
| Attendee | Personal portal with compact header/tabs and event-registration cards | Own accessible registrations, payment meaning, permitted next actions and event help. |
| Director | Club portal with persistent club/year identity and club task navigation | Own club roster, club records, reports, event registrations and capability-gated club tools. |
| Area Coordinator | Club oversight portal with report/filter context and read-only club views | Active clubs available to this role, record/report follow-up and specific authorized review tasks. |
| System administrator | Full platform workbench with grouped persistent navigation | Events, access, shared setup, system settings and cross-event operational work. |

The examples share IMSDA identity, type, controls and accessible interaction patterns. They do not share an administrator menu or a universal dashboard.

## Accuracy details

- The ordinary attendee example has no club/coordinator/staff grant. Multiple people on a registration do not establish a permanent household or guardian authority.
- The attendee financial example is a synthetic direct-billing Family Retreat. A waitlisted or cancelled record is not shown as a payable balance; that presentation follows the existing domain meaning and demonstrates the correction tracked in #1100, not a deployment claim.
- The director's Pathfinder Camporee example is billed to the sponsoring church, with no director Pay now action. Club forms, Monthly Records and event registration remain distinct workflows.
- The current coordinator query includes all active clubs. The sample says “All active clubs available to this role”; it does not invent an enforced area/ministry filter. Planned ministry scope remains separate work.
- Coordinator club/report views are read-only. The existing Team approvals workflow is a narrow exception for qualifying requests at the coordinator's own event locations; it is not a generic club/report approval authority. New-club application decisions remain with system administrators.
- Only the administrator example contains platform setup/access/system controls. A real person's context switch would offer only workspaces they already have authority to enter.
- The design-review link and comparison gallery sit outside the product navigation. None of the sample accounts receives a universal role switcher.
