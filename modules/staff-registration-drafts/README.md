# Staff registration drafts (#1510)

Registrations staff start for someone by phone, on paper or at the door. A draft is **not** a registration: it holds answers, never a payment.

- **What a draft stores:** the event, the form, the published form version it was started on, the source (phone, paper, walk-in), the acting staff member, and registration-scope answers only. Answers go through the browser-draft rule (`modules/forms/public-draft.ts`, "save less"): medical, consent and acknowledgment, signature, payment, birth-date, long-text, address and notes fields are never kept, and unknown keys are dropped on every save. On a roster form, staff also enter the people attending (`attendees`, `[{ clientId, responses }]`, at most the roster's maximum and never more than 50). Each person keeps only what the browser-draft rule keeps for an attendee: names, attendee type, shirt size and paid or limited choices. A birth date, age, medical answer or acknowledgment is the registrant's to give. People with no answer left are dropped. The people are cleared on submit and on discard, and changing them withdraws a sent link like any other change.
- **Who may use it:** `MANAGE_REGISTRATION` on the event, on the server, for every route under `app/api/events/[eventId]/staff-registration-drafts/`. Club-audience events and forms with no published version are refused.
- **Check:** `checkPublicRegistration` (`modules/forms/public-repository.ts`) runs the whole public submit in its own Serializable transaction and rolls it back at the end, so validation, price windows, promo codes, capacity and the waitlist decision are the public form's own. Nothing runs after commit because nothing commits.
- **Hand-off:** one staff action per draft queues one transactional email (template `STAFF_REGISTRATION_DRAFT_LINK`, no event, account sender). The body holds a sentinel; the token is minted when the message is delivered and only its SHA-256 is stored (`link-email.ts`), as for club form links. The link is `/register/finish/[token]`, valid 14 days. Editing the draft, sending again or discarding it withdraws the earlier link.
- **Final submit:** the registrant opens the ordinary public form prefilled from the draft (the answers and, on a roster form, the people) and submits it through the ordinary public route with `staffDraftToken`. Inside the same transaction as the registration, the draft is locked, checked (handed off, unexpired, same event and form), linked to the registration, cleared of answers and people and marked submitted. The same idempotency key replays; a new key on a submitted draft is refused.
- **Staff finish:** staff can also send the registration themselves (`/people/staff-drafts/[draftId]/finish`), with a permissioned override and a reason when a rule would refuse it. The finish page prefills the answers and the people too.
- **Status words:** a draft's chip uses the approved status words (site rule B12): Draft while it is being entered or after the link is sent, Submitted once sent, Cancelled when discarded. What happened ("Link sent to the registrant", "Sent by staff (name)", "Discarded") is plain text beside the chip.
- **Event deletion:** an open or sent draft blocks deleting its event (it counts as an unfinished phone or paper registration). Submitted and discarded drafts are deleted with the event.
- **Not built:** anything in bulk, club events.
