# Staff registration drafts (#1510, first slice)

Registrations staff start for someone by phone, on paper or at the door. A draft is **not** a registration: it holds answers, never a payment.

- **What a draft stores:** the event, the form, the published form version it was started on, the source (phone, paper, walk-in), the acting staff member, and registration-scope answers only. Answers go through the browser-draft rule (`modules/forms/public-draft.ts`, "save less"): medical, consent and acknowledgment, signature, payment, birth-date, long-text, address and notes fields are never kept, and unknown keys are dropped on every save. Attendee-roster answers are not part of a draft; the registrant adds the people.
- **Who may use it:** `MANAGE_REGISTRATION` on the event, on the server, for every route under `app/api/events/[eventId]/staff-registration-drafts/`. Club-audience events and forms with no published version are refused.
- **Check:** `checkPublicRegistration` (`modules/forms/public-repository.ts`) runs the whole public submit in its own Serializable transaction and rolls it back at the end, so validation, price windows, promo codes, capacity and the waitlist decision are the public form's own. Nothing runs after commit because nothing commits.
- **Hand-off:** one staff action per draft queues one transactional email (template `STAFF_REGISTRATION_DRAFT_LINK`, no event, account sender). The body holds a sentinel; the token is minted when the message is delivered and only its SHA-256 is stored (`link-email.ts`), as for club form links. The link is `/register/finish/[token]`, valid 14 days. Editing the draft, sending again or discarding it withdraws the earlier link.
- **Final submit:** the registrant opens the ordinary public form prefilled from the draft and submits it through the ordinary public route with `staffDraftToken`. Inside the same transaction as the registration, the draft is locked, checked (handed off, unexpired, same event and form), linked to the registration, cleared of answers and marked submitted. The same idempotency key replays; a new key on a submitted draft is refused.
- **Not in this slice:** a permissioned override with a reason, staff submitting on the registrant's behalf (acknowledgments and signatures stay the registrant's), roster rows in drafts, anything in bulk, club events.
