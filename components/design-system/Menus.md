The small floating panels that open from a button: the staff account popover, a row's "More" menu and the calendar's "Add to my calendar" list. `ActionsMenu` and `Dialog` have their own parts; the staff shell's menu line sheet is described in `StaffShell`.

- **Popover frame:** `.header-popover` is a white card with a 1px `--line` border, 15px corners, 15px padding and a large soft navy shadow. It is placed 10px under its `.menu-anchor` and aligned to the right edge, with a 275px width. `.account-popover` makes it up to 260px wide and never wider than the screen minus 32px. Text inside is a bold navy `strong`, a `--muted` `p` and a purple link.
- **Account popover:** it is a small labelled dialog, not an ARIA menu. A sticky head holds "Your account" and a 44px **Close** button, so Close stays in view when the list scrolls; the popover scrolls inside the screen height. Below are the person's name and email (with a 48px round photo beside them when they have one), "Database-backed staff session", then 44px links: Edit profile, Add a profile photo (or Change profile photo), the "Switch workspace" group when the person holds more than one workspace, Create event for system administrators, Preview attendee experience, and Sign out. The workspace the person is in is marked with a navy tint and a 4px gold edge. The trigger is a 44px `.avatar` button holding the 36px round photo or an icon.
- **Row "More" menu:** a `.secondary-button` "More" opens `.team-more-menu-list`, a white list with 10px corners, at least 11.5rem wide, whose rows are full-width buttons with 8px corners (`role="menuitem"`). Hover or focus tints a row `#eef4f6`; the destructive item has class `danger` and red text. Arrow keys move between rows.
- **Calendar list:** `.calendar-subscribe-options` is a list of 44px rows (Google Calendar, Outlook.com, Outlook (work or school), Apple Calendar, ICS link, Copy link) in a white 12px-radius card, up to 340px wide. `.calendar-subscribe-popover` places it under the "Add to my calendar" button, right-aligned, up to the screen width minus 24px.
- On a phone, the registration form's attendee editor opens as a full-screen sheet (above 768px it is a card on the page). A form-builder phone preview has its own sheet. Neither is part of this system yet.
